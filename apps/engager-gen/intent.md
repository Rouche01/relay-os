# Intent — CommunityEngager-lite

Help people in allowlisted fashion subreddits (r/malefashionadvice, r/femalefashionadvice) with genuinely useful replies.

Constraints:
- Stay on the allowlist — never post outside those subs.
- No promotional links in drafts.
- Replies should fit the thread, sound human, and be specific (soft judge).
- A human must approve before any comment is posted.
- Default to dry-run; live post only after explicit operator intent later.
- Bound fan-out with a small maxJobs budget.

Success signal (outcome, measured later): approved comments earn a meaningful score within ~48h.
